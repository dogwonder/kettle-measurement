Robin’s 12-month vaccinations are now due. Please phone 01632 960123 to book an appointment.

Vaccines are the best and safest way to protect your child from a range of infectious diseases. It’s important that your child has all the recommended vaccines at the right time to offer them the best protection.

Your child needs three vaccinations now:

The MenB booster vaccine. This vaccine protects against meningococcal group B bacteria (germs) which cause meningitis and septicaemia. The MenB vaccine is called Bexsero; further information is available in the Patient Information Leaflet.

The PCV booster vaccine. This vaccine protects against pneumococcal bacteria which cause meningitis, septicaemia (blood poisoning), pneumonia (lung infection), and ear infections. The PCV vaccine is called Prevenar 13; further information is available in the Patient Information Leaflet.

The first dose of MMRV vaccine. This vaccine protects against measles, mumps, rubella and chickenpox (also called varicella). There are 2 MMRV vaccines which work equally well, called ProQuad and Priorix Tetra. ProQuad contains porcine gelatine and Priorix Tetra does not. If you want your child to have the vaccine without porcine gelatine, ask your practice nurse or GP. Further information is available in the patient information leaflets:

ProQuad

Priorix Tetra

If your child missed any of their routine vaccine appointments so far, please contact your GP practice who will check what they have missed and explain how they will bring your child up to date as soon as possible.

The vaccinations are free of charge.
