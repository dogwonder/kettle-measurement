Vaccines are the best and safest way to protect your child from a range of infectious diseases. Its important that your child has all the recommended vaccines at the right time to offer them the best protection.

Your child needs two vaccinations now:

The combined 6 in 1 vaccine. This vaccine protects against diphtheria, pertussis, polio, tetanus, Haemophilus influenzae B (Hib) and hepatitis B. There are two vaccines which work equally well. Further information is available in the Patient Information Leaflets.

A second dose of MMRV vaccine. This vaccine protects against measles, mumps, rubella and chickenpox (also called varicella). There are two MMRV vaccines which work equally well, called ProQuad and Priorix Tetra. ProQuad contains porcine gelatine and Priorix Tetra does not. If you want your child to have the vaccine without porcine gelatine, ask your practice nurse or GP. Further information is available in the patient information leaflets:

You can read the guide to the MMRV vaccine here: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mmrv-vaccination

If your child missed any of their routine vaccine appointments so far please contact your GP practice who will check what they have missed and explain how they will bring your child up to date as soon as possible.

The vaccinations are free of charge.
