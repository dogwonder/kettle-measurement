Service charge information

The charge covers the shared entrance, lighting and grounds. The annual total describes the whole charging year. The instalment is the smaller sum requested in this letter.

The entrance lighting is inspected during the regular maintenance visit. The grounds schedule covers the shared paths and planted areas. Individual household electricity use is outside this account.

The statement separates the annual charge from the first instalment. Both figures refer to the same property and charging year. Previous correspondence may show a different appointment, which is cancelled in this letter.
